More About Cooper

Cooper is a 6-year-old Brittany Spaniel mix with a lot of personality. Brittany Spaniels were originally bred in France as hunting dogs, and Cooper still has those instincts — he loves sniffing everything on walks and could spend hours exploring a field. He is energetic and playful but also knows how to relax. His favorite spot in the house is a sunny patch on the floor, and he is always happy to curl up next to you on the couch after a long walk. Cooper is very loyal and follows his humans everywhere. He is friendly with strangers and other dogs and rarely gets scared of anything — except the vacuum cleaner. Nobody knows exactly why, but the vacuum is Cooper's one true nemesis. He hears it and immediately runs to hide in another room. The household rule is that Cooper gets moved to a safe spot before any vacuuming happens. It is a small accommodation for a very good dog. Overall, Cooper is the kind of dog who makes every day brighter just by being in it. He is always happy, always loving, and always ready for whatever comes next — as long as it does not involve a vacuum.
