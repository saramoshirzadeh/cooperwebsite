More About Cooper

Cooper is a really good dog. He is a Brittany Spaniel mix which means he has a lot of energy but in the best way possible. He loves being outside and will sniff everything on a walk every single time no matter how many times he has been on the same route. Inside the house he is super relaxed and will find a sunny spot on the floor and hang out there for hours. He is not needy at all and just likes being in the same room as you without being on top of you. He gets along with pretty much everyone including strangers, other dogs, and kids. The only thing that bothers him is loud noises but honestly who can blame him. Overall Cooper is just one of those dogs that makes everything better without even trying. He is not doing anything crazy, he is just a really great and loyal companion who brings a lot of happiness to everyone around him. If you have ever met a Brittany Spaniel mix before then you already know how special they are, and Cooper is no exception to that.
