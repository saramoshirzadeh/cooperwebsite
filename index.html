About Me

Hi! This is Cooper. He is a 6 year old Brittany Spaniel mix and one of the best dogs you will ever meet. He is super friendly and loves being around people, just don't expect him to cuddle because he really likes his personal space. He is pretty relaxed most of the time and loves hanging out on the couch next to you. He goes on walks every day and takes them very seriously. Every single smell needs to be investigated, no exceptions. He is not scared of much and is pretty brave for his size. The one thing that gets him is loud noises, he is just not a fan. Other than that he is easy going and fun to be around. He does not ask for much, just some food, a good walk, and a spot on the couch. If you want to know more about Cooper check out the About page!
