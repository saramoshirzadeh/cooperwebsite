About Me

Hi! My name is Cooper and I am a 6-year-old Brittany Spaniel mix. I am a happy, energetic dog who loves going on walks, playing fetch, and cuddling on the couch. My Brittany Spaniel roots make me naturally curious and always ready for an adventure. I love sniffing everything on my walks and chasing squirrels in the backyard. At 6 years old I have settled into my favorite routines — morning walks, afternoon naps in sunny spots, and evening cuddles. I am very loyal and love spending time with the people I care about. I follow my humans from room to room just to be close to them. I am also pretty brave about most things — loud noises, strangers, big dogs — none of that bothers me much. There is, however, one thing that I absolutely cannot handle: the vacuum cleaner. The moment it comes out of the closet I disappear to the farthest room in the house. It is a perfectly reasonable response and I stand by it. Other than that one totally understandable fear, I am an easygoing, loving dog who brings a lot of joy to everyone around me. I hope you enjoy learning more about me on this website!
